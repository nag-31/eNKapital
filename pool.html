TL;DR
In plain English

A Lighter Public Pool is like an on-chain managed account. You deposit USDC. I trade it using the same rules I teach. When you withdraw, the smart contract automatically calculates your share and pays you out. No private signals to copy. No DMs. No paywall. Just on-chain execution you can verify.

How it works

Three Steps. No Surprises.

01

You Deposit USDC

Connect a wallet on Lighter, open the pool page, and deposit any amount of USDC you're comfortable with. The pool tracks your share automatically. Your funds stay on-chain — never custodied by me.

02

I Trade The Rules

I run the strategy on the pooled capital — the same rules I publish in the Trader's Rules series, applied to the same setups you see on the live positions dashboard. I'm trading my own money in the same pool, so my incentives match yours.

03

Withdraw. Profits Split Automatically.

Whenever you want out, withdraw your share. The smart contract calculates your gain, deducts the operator fee from profits only, and sends the rest back to your wallet. No reconciliation, no waiting on me.
