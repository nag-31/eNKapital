Trader's Rules

The Rules That Separate
Good Traders From Bad.

Every rule below comes from a real mistake, a real loss, or a real moment of clarity. Steal them.

08 Rules Published Updated Weekly
Rule · #01

The 5-Minute Rule

Never enter a trade in the first 5 minutes of a session. Let the noise settle before you commit.

Discipline
Rule · #02

Size Your Position

Never risk more than 1–2% of your account on a single trade. Survive first. Win later.

Risk
Rule · #03

No Trading After 3 Losses

Three losses in a row means your head isn't right. Close the chart. Walk away.

Psychology
Rule · #04

Confirm Before Entry

Wait for confirmation. A setup isn't valid until the market actually does the thing.

Discipline
Rule · #05

Never Short The Strongest Coin

Momentum is a force. Don't fight it — ride the weakest, short the broken.

Discipline
Rule · #06

Cut Losses Early

A small loss today beats a big loss tomorrow. Hope is not a trade management strategy.

Risk
Rule · #07

The Revenge Trade

A loss triggers pain. Your brain wants to trade back to feel better. That's not a setup. Don't.

Psychology
Rule · #08

Never Move Your Stop Loss

Your stop was set by the calm version of you. The version watching it tick is not in charge.
