The Story · Naga Katika

I spent years making the same mistakes that most retail traders make. Moving stop losses. Taking revenge trades after a loss. Chasing pumps and fighting strong trends.

eNKapital started as a personal journal — a set of rules I wrote for myself after blowing accounts that didn't need to be blown. Each one came from a screenshot, a regret, or a moment of "I'll never do that again."

The Trader's Rules series is that journal, turned into short-form content. Every rule is under 75 seconds. Every rule comes from a real loss, a real moment of clarity, or a real conversation with another trader who was bleeding.

If even one rule saves you from one bad trade, this was worth building.

The Series

Short-Form. Brutally Practical.

The Trader's Rules series is a weekly short-form format. One rule per video. Under 75 seconds each. Built for the feed, not the seminar.

Every rule comes from real trading experience — yours might already be one of them. The format is the same every week: hook, rule, the mistake it prevents, and the move that replaces it.
